Play Provenance Repair

Repair Play Store installer/initiator metadata for an app you legitimately own, and detect a known stale Play-protection warning state.

Selective by design This does not scan every package with dumpsys. Search is performed from the manager's cached app list when available, and only the selected package is inspected. Repair may perform an in-place reinstall of that app's currently installed APKs; app data is preserved. A separate stale-warning cleanup is only offered when PAIRIP is detected, provenance is already fully Play, and an eFuse=true preference is actually present.
